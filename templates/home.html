Project Introduction

EduShield is an advanced administrative intelligence tool that leverages data science to address one of higher education's most persistent challenges: student attrition. By examining historical cohorts across demographic backgrounds, socioeconomic conditions, and early academic records, our predictive engine calculates real-time retention probabilities, empowering academic advisors to act proactively before a student drops out.

Predictive Risk Engine

Analyzes student academic and socioeconomic profiles through our trained XGBoost classifier to compute real-time dropout probabilities.

Interactive EDA

Explores student demographics, scholarship parameters, and course attrition metrics via responsive, multi-dimensional Plotly charts.

Targeted Interventions

Provides personalized academic counselling, financial aid advice, and mentoring interventions based on the predicted risk level.

Workflow Overview

Step 1
Input Demographics & Academic Credits

Advisors fill in standard socioeconomic and first/second semester grades.

Step 2
Scaling & Model Inference

The data is scaled and processed by the auto-selected XGBoost model.

Step 3
Report Generation & Interventions

The system displays the risk level, outputs recommendations, and exports a PDF report.
