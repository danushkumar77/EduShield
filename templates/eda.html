Target Retention Distribution
Target Distribution Insight:

The cohort is split: 49.9% have successfully graduated, while 32.1% have dropped out. The remaining 18.0% represent currently enrolled students. Our models evaluate dropout (At Risk) vs graduate/enrolled (Safe).

Gender Distribution vs Retention
Gender Retention Insight:

Male students (1) show a significantly higher dropout rate (approx. 45% of male enrollment) compared to female students (0) (approx. 25% of female enrollment). Gender acts as a key demographic risk flag.

Scholarship Status vs Retention
Scholarship Insight:

Scholarship holders have a remarkably high graduation rate (nearly 75%). Conversely, non-scholarship holders are 3x more likely to drop out. Direct financial aid acts as a powerful protective driver.

Admission Grade Outliers & Distribution
Admission Grade Insight:

Students with higher entry exam scores are statistically clustered in the graduate group. Average admission grades show a clear distinction: graduates average 128/200 vs dropouts averaging 121/200.

Student Age at Enrollment
Age Retention Insight:

Older students (ages 25-50+) are highly correlated with attrition. Traditional students entering right after secondary school (ages 18-20) present much lower dropout probabilities.

Course-wise Enrollment Attrition Split
Course Attrition Insight:

Program selection impacts graduation likelihood. Engineering courses (Informatics Engineering) exhibit higher dropout counts, while professional health qualifications (Nursing) present higher retention.

Feature Correlation Matrix
Correlation Insight:

Academic performance parameters (1st and 2nd semester approved credits and grades) exhibit the highest positive correlation weights. Debtor status is negatively correlated with approved credits, linking financial strain with academic struggles.

Age Box Plots Outlier Diagnostic
Outliers Diagnostic Insight:

The box plot shows significant outliers in the Graduate and Enrolled categories above age 30, whereas the Dropout category features a wider interquartile range extending into older ages, showing age is a broad risk variable.

1st Semester Completed Credits Histograms
Credits Completed Histogram Insight:

Graduates cluster heavily around 6-8 completed units. Students completing 0 units in the first semester are almost entirely comprised of dropouts. Completing fewer than 3-4 credits signals a critical need for academic recovery.
