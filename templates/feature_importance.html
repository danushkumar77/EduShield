How These Features Contribute to Dropout Predictions
Academic Performance

Approved credit units in 1st & 2nd semesters represent the student's ability to cope with curriculum requirements. A drop in approved credits below 3 units in the first year indicates immediate risk of academic withdrawal.

Financial Compliance

Whether a student's tuition fees are up-to-date is a crucial indicator. Debtor status is highly weighted, showing that financial stress and late tuition payments are primary non-academic triggers for dropping out.

Socio-Demographics

Age at enrollment (older non-traditional students show higher dropout statistics) and Scholarship Status (acting as a major protection factor) represent key weights that adjust prediction probability.
